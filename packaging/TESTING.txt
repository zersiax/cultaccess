CultAccess - what to test and what to send back
===============================================

Thank you for testing. This build has a lot that works and a short list of things
that have never been confirmed by anyone except the author, so your report is the
only way some of it gets verified.


THE MOST USEFUL THING YOU CAN DO
--------------------------------

Press F7 whenever something happens that the mod did NOT tell you about.

That writes a numbered marker into the log with your position, the game's current
location and what the game is waiting for. You will hear "Log marker 1", "Log
marker 2" and so on. Afterwards just say "marker 3 was the animation I could not
identify" and the exact moment can be found.

It also writes down every follower standing near you and what each one is doing,
so "a lot of follower animation was happening and I could not tell what" is now
a question a single press answers.



THE LOG
-------

Everything the mod sees is written here:

    <game folder>\BepInEx\LogOutput.log

It is overwritten each time the game starts, so copy it out BEFORE relaunching if
something interesting happened. Sending the whole file is fine and preferred - it
is plain text and there is no need to trim it.


KEYS
----

  F1   help, one section per press. Start here; it lists everything.
  F2   the mod's own settings menu
  F3   how your cult is doing: faith, food, cleanliness and warmth, then how
       many followers you have and how many have died
  F4   describe one follower in full - loyalty, food, health, what is wrong
       with them, what they are doing, traits, marriage, age. Describes the
       follower selected in the target list, or the nearest one
  F5   while placing a building, says which way the nearest cell is that the
       structure will actually fit in. It doesn't move you there, just tells you how to get there yourself.

  F7   mark the log (see above)
  F8   read the open panel
  F9   which speech backend is in use
  F10  stop speech
  F11  repeat the last thing said
  F12  where am I: health, fervour, tarot, day and time, current objective

  [(left bracket) ] and  (right bracket), or page-up and page-down:  previous / next nearby target

  \ (backslash)     rescan surroundings
  / (slash) and ? (shift+slash) (or ctrl+page-up and ctrl+page-down)     change target filter
  ; (semicolon) (or home)     start or stop walking guidance
  ' (quote)     repeat the current direction
  . (period)     nearby enemies
  , (comma)     point the beacon at an enemy
  delete        autowalk: walk the route for you; press again to stop

CONTROLLER
----------

Added in 0.2.0 and still lightly tested, so worth your time if you use a pad.

Hold the LEFT TRIGGER and the pad becomes the mod's hotkeys. The left trigger is
the only button this game leaves unused, so everything is a chord with it held. You can still walk with it held down, but that's all you can do until you let go.


  d-pad up / down       previous / next target
  d-pad left / right    change the target filter (empty ones are skipped)
  A                     start or stop walking guidance
  B                     list nearby enemies
  X                     point the beacon at the next enemy
  Y                     where am I
  left shoulder         rescan
  right shoulder        repeat the walking direction
  left stick click      autowalk
  right stick click     stop speech
  right trigger         repeat the last thing said
  Back                  help
  Start                 settings menu

When a fight starts the target filter moves to the enemies by itself and moves
back when the room is clear, so in combat left and right are already stepping
enemies, and landing on one points the beacon at it.

All of it is rebindable in the ControllerLayer section of the config file.


WHAT IS UNCONFIRMED AND WORTH TRYING
------------------------------------

  1. Preaching a sermon. At the end you should hear which doctrine it fed, how
     much was gained, the progress toward the next upgrade, and how many
     followers attended.

  2. Re-educating a dissenter. Each session should say how much dissent is left
     and roughly how many more daily sessions it needs. It is a seven-day course
     on ONE follower.

  3. A story moment. If the target list goes empty, it should say a story moment
     is in progress and repeat what the game wants, rather than just going quiet.

  4. Non-English. If you play in another language, everything the mod says should
     be in that language. All translations are machine drafts - please flag
     anything a native speaker would object to, especially where the mod's word
     for Shrine, Devotion or Sermon differs from the game's own word.

  5. Twitch, only if you stream. Connect from the pause menu, not the main menu -
     the connection belongs to the save file. During a Help or Hinder vote the
     mod should say chat is voting and that the base gate is held shut, then say
     what chat chose.

  6. Flying worms and the diving maggot miniboss. Both should now buzz a warning
     before they hit you - the worm's telegraph is a full second, the longest of
     any enemy. The maggot's warning is aimed at where it will LAND rather than
     at the maggot, because it crosses the room mid-leap. Neither has ever been
     heard by anyone; they are written from the game's code alone.

  7. A miniboss fight. Two things to listen for. "Room clear" used to fire two or
     three times during one of these, between waves; it should now only be said
     when the room has genuinely stayed empty. And the forward door of that room
     should say it is sealed and that this is the last room of the run, rather
     than being offered as an exit that does nothing.

  8. Spawners. Some enemies generate the others - killing the spawner kills
     everything it made and ends the fight, and its spawn are worth no
     experience. Those are announced as "spawner" and put first in the enemy
     roster and the enemies filter. Tell me if one is missed or mislabelled.

  9. Health during a long fight. Announcements of damage are now spaced out so
     they cannot talk over the combat cues, but anything that is NOT more damage
     - a blue heart, a maximum health increase - should still be announced
     immediately. Silence there is a bug.

  10. F3 gives you bars but I haven't had a chance to see if the notifications for these falling low actually fire correctly yet.


  11. F4, give status of the follower you're targeting when you get to them. There's a lot of moving bits in that readout so particularly localization is a great thing to test.

  12. Give the follower cards a good once-over. Anything sound weird, let me know.

  13. Beeping pickups should now also show up in the pathfinder. If not, send me the log so I can fix :)

WHAT TO INCLUDE IN A REPORT
---------------------------

  - What you did, roughly.
  - What you heard, and what you expected instead.
  - The marker number if you pressed F7.
  - LogOutput.log.

"It said nothing when I did X" is a completely valid and useful report. Silence is
a bug in this kind of mod, and it is the hardest kind to find without you.


KNOWN ROUGH EDGES
-----------------

  - Some announcements are still English regardless of your game language. The
    translation migration is partial and known to be so.
  - Controller support is new and only the left-trigger layer is covered. The
    mod's own settings menu still reads the keyboard only, so Start will open it
    but the pad will not navigate it.
  - If the mod says at startup that your audio output is not stereo, believe it.
    Windows can report a surround output on headphones - sometimes after spatial
    sound has been switched back OFF - and the game's positional sounds then go
    to speakers that do not exist while music keeps playing.
  - Auto Cook and the Tailor minigame have never been tested by anyone.
  - Fishing has no audio cues at all yet. Casting is the same shape as cooking
    and will be cued; reeling is a marker you have to hold inside a moving
    section, which is a different problem and is being designed rather than
    bolted on. Reported and scoped, not forgotten.
